## How to read this

- **Vol (US)** = national Google Ads monthly volume for the exact string (best signal for geo-modified terms — nearly all searchers of "patio covers chandler az" are local).
- **Vol (AZ)** = Arizona-state monthly volume (best signal for generic head terms like "pergola", "patio covers").
- **KD** = keyword difficulty 0–100 (lower = easier). `n/a` = DataForSEO had no KD sample (typically ultra-low-volume long-tail).
- **CPC** = advertiser cost-per-click (proxy for commercial/lead value — high CPC = high-value buyer intent).
- Hyper-local exact strings (e.g. "pergola builder chandler az") return **null volume** in Google Ads — this is NORMAL for city+service long-tail. They are still HIGH-INTENT money pages; volume shows up under the head term + "near me" instead. Build the page anyway.

> **Strategic read:** The metro is a low-difficulty market. Almost every commercial term sits at **KD 0–16**. There is no content moat — the top local rankers are thin (see competitors.md). Money is in the **high-CPC service pages** ($10–$29 CPC on pergola/patio-cover install terms) captured by city; informational volume (pergola ideas / kits / cost) is a cheap top-of-funnel layer at KD 0–1.
