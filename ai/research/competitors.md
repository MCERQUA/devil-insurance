## Content gaps we can beat

The entire local field is **thin homepages + a few light service-area pages, ranking primarily on GBP reviews rather than content depth**. Only royalcovers.com has real content structure. Gaps to exploit:

1. **Nobody owns the cost/pricing question.** Every PAA is cost-by-size ("20x20", "12x20", "10x20") + Arizona permitting — yet no local ranker has a proper cost page with a size→price table. **Build a "Patio Cover & Pergola Cost in Chandler/Phoenix" page** with a 10x10/10x20/12x20/20x20 pricing table + material cost comparison (alumawood vs wood vs louvered). Instantly wins the PAA box.

2. **Dedicated city × service landing pages** beat thin homepages. Only sonoranshade + outdoorelements run true Chandler pages; most competitors rank a generic homepage. Build a full matrix: {Chandler, Gilbert, Mesa, Queen Creek, Tempe} × {pergola, patio cover, alumawood, louvered, ramada, shade sail}. Each 800–1,200 words.

3. **Beat royalcovers on depth on the flagship pages.** Match their winning blocks (Installation Process · Custom Design · Warranty · HOA Approvals · Transparent Pricing · Desert-Engineered) and add what they lack: an FAQ block answering the 6 PAA questions, a real size-based price table, and material comparison tables. Target 1,500–2,000+ words on hub pages.

4. **"Arizona permit for a pergola/patio cover" content is missing** despite appearing in PAA on both cost SERPs. A Chandler/Maricopa-County permitting explainer captures a PAA slot no competitor answers and builds trust ("we pull permits + get HOA approvals" — royalcovers only teases HOA).

5. **Louvered / adjustable pergolas are under-served locally.** "louvered pergola" (14,800 US, KD 0) and "adjustable louvered pergola" (1,300, KD 0) have almost no dedicated local pages (only Royal's "Equinox Louvered Roofs" H3 mention). A dedicated louvered/motorized pergola product page is open field.

6. **Commercial / institutional shade is wide open.** "playground shade structure" (590, KD 0, $13.65 CPC), "commercial shade structure" (390, KD 15), "parking lot shade" — no local residential competitor targets these high-CPC B2B terms. A commercial-shade section captures a whole buyer segment competitors ignore.

7. **Ramadas ("ramada" 2,400 AZ, LOW comp)** — a distinctly Arizona term with weak competition and no dedicated local pages. Own it with a custom-ramada service page.

8. **Quality/polish edge:** phxpatios has typos, azsuncovers is 366 words. A clean, fast, schema-marked (LocalBusiness + Service + FAQ), image-rich site with genuine depth will out-signal the incumbents on the ~4 winnable organic slots per SERP while GBP review velocity is built in parallel.

### Recommended flagship page blueprint (to beat Royal Covers)
`H1: [Service] in [City], AZ` → Intro (desert-engineered value prop) → **Products We Build** (alumawood / lattice / solid / louvered / wood / ramada) → **Our Process** (design→permit/HOA→pricing→install) → **Cost & Size Table** (10x10…20x20) → **Why Choose Us** (licensed ROC#, warranty, HOA approvals, transparent pricing) → **Project Gallery** (captioned) → **Service Area** (Chandler + metro links) → **FAQ** (the 6 PAA questions + AZ permit) → Reviews/social proof → CTA (free estimate). Target 1,500–2,000 words + LocalBusiness/Service/FAQ schema.
